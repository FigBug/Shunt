0.0.5:
- New physics engine: 2D contact detection with a track-constrained impulse
  solver — smoother pushing, wedging and collisions, especially at switches
- New map: Toronto
- The Shunt Level Editor now ships in the installers
- Maps load from a shared, writable folder (installed there and editable in the
  editor) instead of being built into the app

0.0.4:
- Free cars no longer pile up on the same spot
- Score sound plays once per shipment instead of once per car
- Collision sound has a 2-second cooldown per engine
- Engines no longer couple a car sitting on the other branch of a switch

0.0.3:
- Final standings screen ranks every player from winner to loser
- New maps: Chicago and New York (removed Mobile)
- Audio: engine rumble, horn, and couple/uncouple/collision/score cues
- Scoring streaks reward delivering a whole consist together
- Smarter AI: reliably delivers, backs off from deadlocks, and no longer
  fights other engines over the same track
- Fixed cars jumping to the other track after collisions
- Engines can no longer overlap each other

0.0.2:
- Level editor for authoring track, switches, spawns and drop-offs
- Title-screen map selector; remembers the last-played map
- Esc returns to the main menu
- Level-wide car count spread across spawn points, avoiding switches

0.0.1:
- Initial version
